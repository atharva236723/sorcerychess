The Grimoire

The Four Spellcasters

Beyond the classical army, every side fields four arcane pieces — each one slides like a Queen, and each one bends a single rule of chess. Behold their sigils, learn their craft, and watch the magic move on a living board.

✶

The Wizard

Hexcaster · poison
Queen movement Poisons at range 4 charges 2-move recharge

The Wizard fights without ever touching his prey. He sights the first enemy along any of his eight Queen-lines, flings a curse, and swaps places with it in a blink — leaving the victim doomed to rot from within.

  • 1Click a blue-glowing enemy on one of the Wizard's lines to cast.
  • 2The target is poisoned and the two pieces trade squares instantly.
  • 3After the victim's owner makes two moves, the poison kills it outright.
  • 4Four casts per game; he must recharge for two moves between spells.
Live spell
⚗

The Alchemist

Healer · cure
Queen movement Cures allies Unlimited uses Swaps places

The Wizard's mirror image. Where one corrupts, the Alchemist mends. She reaches the first poisoned ally on any of her lines, brews an antidote, and trades places with the piece she saves — the counter-spell that keeps a cursed Queen alive.

  • 1Click a blue-glowing poisoned ally on one of her lines.
  • 2The poison is purged and the piece is safe again.
  • 3The Alchemist and the healed piece swap squares.
  • 4No limit — she can cure as often as a target presents itself.
Live spell
⚚

The Witch

Necromancer · revive
Queen movement Raises the dead Self-sacrifice From the graveyard

No piece is ever truly gone — and the Witch is why. She reaches into your graveyard, pulls a fallen piece back onto her own square, and crumbles to dust in its place. One life given for another raised: the most dramatic trade on the board.

  • 1Use the panel's revive button, then pick a fallen piece from your graveyard.
  • 2That piece returns to life on the Witch's square.
  • 3The Witch dies to pay for it — a sacrifice, not a free summon.
  • 4Revive is sealed while your King is in check.
Live spell
❅

The Freezer

Frostbinder · freeze
Queen movement Never captures Freezes 3 moves Then cooldown

The Freezer slides like a Queen but never takes a piece. Instead it seals the first enemy on its line in ice for three moves — even a Queen — and swaps places with the frozen piece. A frozen piece can't move and casts no threat: time itself becomes your weapon.

  • 1Click a blue-glowing enemy on one of the Freezer's lines.
  • 2The target is frozen for three moves — locked and harmless.
  • 3The Freezer and the frozen piece swap squares.
  • 4After casting, the Freezer must cool down before it can freeze again.
Live spell
