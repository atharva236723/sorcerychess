About us

Sorcery Chess started with a simple question: what would chess feel like if the board were bigger and magic were real? The answer is a 12×12 battlefield where the classic army marches beside four spellcasters — pieces that bend the oldest rules of the game without breaking what makes chess feel like chess.

The game

Every side fields the full classical set plus the Wizard (poisons an enemy from a distance), the Alchemist (cures a cursed ally), the Freezer (locks a piece in ice for three moves — even a queen), and the Witch (raises the dead from the graveyard at the cost of her own life). Add a five-minute clock with increments and no piece ever truly leaving the table, and familiar openings turn into something entirely new. Prefer the classics? A Classic mode offers pure 8×8 chess on a standard board, no spellcasters, same engine.

How to play

There is nothing to install and no setup to fuss over. Open the game, choose your battle — duel a friend on the same device, play online over a short room code, or challenge five tiers of AI from the gentle Apprentice to the merciless Dominion — and the board is arranged for you instantly. Win by checkmate, by capturing the enemy king, or by running the clock to zero. Every victory earns rating points that climb a ladder of seventeen ranks, from Junior to The Monarch.

How it's built

The whole game — board, rules, 3D camera, sound — is hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with zero dependencies, no framework and no build step. The strict black & white board keeps the focus on the duel; the only colours you'll see are the blue of magic and the red of a king in danger. Your games save themselves in your browser, so a refresh never costs you a match.

Get in touch

Found a bug, beaten a friend with a brilliant Witch sacrifice, or got an idea for a fifth spellcaster? Head to the contact page — we'd love to hear it.
